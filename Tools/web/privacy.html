Privacy Policy

BoomYard · Last updated 24 October 2026

BoomYard is a game. It does not ask for, collect or sell personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, purchases you made in the game) and your settings are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser data deletes them.

Purchases

If you buy coins in the app, the payment is processed by Apple or Google; the game never sees your payment details. It only receives a confirmation that the purchase went through.

No accounts, no tracking

The game has no sign-in and no advertising identifiers, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

BoomYard is rated 16+. It is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on the BoomYard project page or contact the developer through the store listing.
